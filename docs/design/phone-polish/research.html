RESEARCH / 21 NOVEMBER 2026 · REVISION 2 / 0.4.6 FEEDBACK

Make the next action clear. Keep the choices in view.

Recommendation: Keep Miqat’s identity. Change the interaction structure: a warm welcome, labeled location, one permission decision, full-page style previews, visible small choice sets, inline numbers, a unified prayer schedule and a discoverable widget editor.

What this research can establish

This is desk research using current official product pages, product help and platform guidance, grounded in the owner’s initial eleven observations and sixteen follow-up points. Local 0.4.6 source and existing native screenshots were inspected for context. It is not a new hands-on audit of competitor apps, a usability study, or a claim that a popular app’s choices are all good. Existing captures are baseline references, not fresh native acceptance of this proposal.

The reviewed product is the Android phone app on feature/companion-installation, 0.4.6/code15. The main checkout still has an older companion. No production UI, persistence, permission, scheduler or TV transport code is changed by these mockups.

Behavior decisions to settle before implementation

Location and permission

Detecting location fills country and nearest city while keeping coordinates authoritative. It must not silently replace an accurate position with the city center. Country/city can be changed manually. If detection cannot map to a supported city or country, retain the coordinates and show an honest detected-location label; never fabricate a city. The current city catalog has country labels but needs a stable grouping strategy for a country picker. Do not add runtime geocoding/network access as an incidental fix.

Confirm location saves the confirmed location once. Android 13+ may show its notification prompt only when appropriate. Denial or dismissal advances setup without scheduling alerts, asking for exact-alarm access, or retrying the prompt. Older Android versions have no equivalent runtime notification dialog: inspect actual OS status and preserve voluntary opt-out in Settings. Existing granted permissions must not be reset during an upgrade. Exact-alarm and full-screen access are separate, and full screen stays optional. Declining notifications must also suppress scheduled audio even though platform media-session behavior can differ.

One prayer alert model

The switch beside a prayer name enables its alerts. Off disables the entire configuration. Enabled prayers have three sound choices: Adhan (selected full recording), Short tone, or Silent. Vibration is independent; the proposed default is two short pulses. “Silent” means no sound; vibration follows its own switch. There is no competing “notification” sound mode. Permission and Android channel settings still govern delivery. The optional full-screen prayer presentation has a normal toggle; it does not enable notifications or audio.

Each prayer owns its repeat weekdays, timing, voice and length. Day overrides contain only sound and vibration differences, with a Use prayer defaults switch. Disabled prayers and excluded weekdays stay off regardless of overrides. This replaces the earlier global time-window editor in the proposal. It covers a weekday work routine; it does not infer work/home, use geofences, or claim time-of-day rules. Existing quiet-hour rules cannot be silently discarded: migration and before/after reminder boundaries must be agreed before implementation.

Adhan length is 5–100% of the selected recording, with an explicit ending-preview button. The gallery plays the approved sample recording from five seconds before the selected cutoff. Moving the slider never starts sound. Production must use the actually selected recording, including imported audio, resolve its duration and stop on navigation. Vibration effects need device support; use a basic two-pulse fallback instead of offering unverified patterns. See Android haptics APIs.

Calendar and Qibla evidence limits

The interactive calendar uses the browser’s Umm al-Qura calendar for real month lengths and date navigation. Displayed prayer times are deliberately illustrative fixtures (one-minute daily shifts), not astronomical calculations or an approved timetable. Native implementation must use Miqat’s calculation engine, timezone, Hijri adjustment and agreed calendar convention. Test month/year transitions, DST and date boundaries there.

The owner’s QiblaFind reference was inspected as a map interaction reference. Our gallery loads four actual OpenStreetMap tiles only after Load map, with attribution and a calculated Cairo-to-Kaaba route. No device location or IP-geolocation lookup is requested; Cairo is demo data. Map requests disclose IP and viewed area to the provider. Production should allow manual city/coordinates, opt-in approximate detection, a sensor-unavailable map fallback and honest accuracy guidance. Choose a provider and review its privacy/usage terms before shipping; retain offline compass/bearing. OSM tile policy requires attribution, normal caching and no bulk/offline prefetch. This mock is not an offline tile package or production service commitment.

Readable controls and honest support

Use the existing Miqat palette, Alexandria, Marcellus, canonical icons and portrait Signature art. Recessed/raised appearance is restrained: a selected mark and crisp outline carry meaning, rather than shadows alone. At large text sizes, labels may wrap and rows may grow. Keep numbers and +/- together; do not shrink labels or targets to force one-line layout.

Offer supported TV systems, not an invented model database. The repository implements Android/Google TV and LG webOS; it does not certify every model of either family. Existing unsupported Samsung/Roku/Other entries are removed from the proposal. Discovery/connection must still check actual capabilities. LG limits, physical approval, pinned trust, explicit install, downgrade checks and recovery remain visible.

Page coverage and boundaries

The gallery covers welcome/setup, location pickers and failures, permission outcomes, home, Qibla and sensor failure, adhan screen, every phone settings section, all five prayer editors, schedule and validation, voice/custom import, widget layouts and pin outcomes, TV setup/pairing, Android installer subflows, LG install, connected/offline TVs and TV settings categories and editors. Repeated catalog items use one shared picker pattern. These are English review fixtures, not newly shipped product pages.

Current companion route families map as follows: prayers → Prayer home; phone-settings → phone settings and sections; tvs → empty/paired TVs; pair → scan/manual/approval; overview → remote; settings → TV categories; editor → short-choice/custom/number patterns; appearance → TV appearance; updates → honest update status; preferences → language/preferences; lg-setup → LG preparation/trust/install; tv-setup → platform/source; onboarding → welcome/location/style; qibla → compass/sensor failure; widgets → inventory/editor/pin.

Android OS screens (permission Settings, document picker, debugging approval, widget placement and system uninstall) remain OS-owned. Their prompts are illustrated or identified as handoffs, not pixel-perfect replicas. Camera, live Qibla heading, real prayer calculations, scheduled audio, transport, persisted draft state and installation are simulated. Explicit ending preview uses approved audio and Load map requests actual tiles. Core mock interactions demonstrate navigation, short-choice selection, steppers, weekday toggles, visual themes, search and widget shape/opacity. Catalog pickers and rails show representative entries; implementation must retain the full canonical lists. Picker values and saved settings are fixtures rather than persisted app state.

Implementation sequence after design review

  1. Welcome, country/city, detection result and notification-denial journey; preserve onboarding resume and prior permission state.
  2. Shared phone controls and full-page appearance draft preview; then widgets entry/customization and pin fallback.
  3. Per-prayer sound/vibration/day policy and explicit legacy migration, with timing-access fallback, DST and reminder-boundary checks.
  4. Supported-only TV chooser and matching compact controls in phone TV pages; retain installation, trust and recovery behavior.
  5. Native English/Arabic/RTL and large-text checks, actual scheduled playback and real Android permission/launcher journeys. Human physical TV/phone acceptance is separate.

No release bump, APK, merge, physical deployment, new dependency, new artwork, account, analytics, geofence or cloud feature belongs to this research pass.
